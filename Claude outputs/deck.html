Data handling

Your files never stick around.

Built to be safe with sensitive material by default — nothing is kept longer than it takes to answer your question.

No database

Never written to permanent storage

Documents are processed in memory and a temporary session folder only — nothing lands in a persistent database.

Auto-wiped

Cleared the moment your session ends

Close the tab, refresh the page, or quit the app — everything you uploaded is deleted immediately.

On demand

Or clear it yourself, any time

One click removes every uploaded document and its conversation, whenever you want — no waiting on a session to end.

What it does well
